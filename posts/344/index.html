AI Agent Learning Resources for Non-Developers

2026-04-11

A selection of introductory resources for people who want to explore AI agents, with priority given to materials that non-developers and people unfamiliar with digital tools can begin using.

The Easiest Places to Start

These resources are suited to people who are almost entirely new to using AI. They emphasize practical examples over technical explanations.

  • ChatGPT Foundations — Free; beginner level; hands-on webinar and example guide. This is one of the easiest free resources to recommend. It is relatively easy to follow without a technical background, and practical use cases come before feature explanations. It lowers the barrier for first-time users, helps people develop a sense of what they can ask, and includes a resource guide that works as a collection of practice examples.
  • ChatGPT Foundations Webinar Resource Guide — Includes examples involving cooking, travel planning, image analysis, and writing. Rather than simply explaining features, it helps users see how AI can be used in practice.
  • AI @ Home — Free; beginner level; everyday, hands-on material. This resource focuses more on daily life than workplace productivity. It covers tasks such as weekly planning, meal planning, email, and understanding documents. It presents AI as an everyday tool rather than an imposing technology, with many examples that are easy to explain to general users.

Google Courses

These are paid or subscription-based introductory courses.

  • Google AI Essentials on Coursera — Paid, although it seems possible to take only the beginning for free; beginner level; general introductory course. It does not go too deeply into technical material, covers both everyday and workplace use, and may help reduce the sense of uncertainty for people encountering AI for the first time.
  • Google Prompting Essentials on Coursera — Paid or subscription-based; beginner-plus level; prompt practice course. This is a good option for people who want to learn more specifically about prompting. It focuses on practicing prompts, helps users examine how they structure questions, and works well as a next-step resource for improving output quality.

Resources Connected to Work and Business

These may be appropriate for people who want to learn how to use AI in business contexts.

  • Microsoft: Transform Business Workflows with Generative AI — Free; beginner-plus level; workplace-focused introductory material. Offered through Microsoft Learn, it is relatively easy to introduce to office workers. Its official page describes it as suitable for beginners and business users, with no coding required. The workplace context is clear, and its focus aligns well with office work, presentation materials, and productivity improvement.
  • ChatGPT 101: Introduction to ChatGPT for Small Businesses — Free; beginner level; hands-on material for small businesses, self-employed people, and small-team operators. It appears to cover generating marketing ideas, writing customer messages, basic data analysis, and support for small-scale operational tasks.
  • Resource Guides for Small Business Videos — Free; beginner-plus level; collection of prompt examples. Topics include marketing ideas, checklist creation, customer communication, image generation, and data analysis.
  • Small Business Prompt Pack — Free; beginner-plus level; template-based prompt collection. Because the prompts are formatted as templates, they are easy to modify and use. They connect directly to day-to-day work and are convenient for quickly reviewing practical examples.

Claude Resources

  • Introduction to Claude Cowork — Free; lower-intermediate level; hands-on material for working with files and projects. It includes workflows involving files and folders and connects well to real tasks such as research, organization, and document work. It shows a way of using AI more as a work companion than as a simple question-and-answer tool. It seems useful for people who have used chatbots before and want to move toward agent-like workflows.
  • Claude 101 — Free; beginner level; introductory material for the Claude product. Its purpose is to let users try Claude’s various services.

Additional Resource

  • AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations — Free; beginner-plus level; concepts and a framework for safe use. It addresses effective and safe use together, focusing more on criteria for judgment than on basic usage instructions alone. It may also be suitable to introduce in educational or public-sector contexts.

At a Glance

  • ChatGPT Foundations — Free; beginner; easiest starting point and a main free introductory recommendation.
  • AI @ Home — Free; beginner; everyday-use examples.
  • Google AI Essentials — Paid or subscription-based; beginner; general course for complete beginners.
  • Google Prompting Essentials — Paid or subscription-based; beginner-plus; useful after gaining some initial experience.
  • Microsoft: Transform Business Workflows with Generative AI — Free; beginner-plus; additional recommendation for office workers.
  • Introduction to Claude Cowork — Free; lower-intermediate; expands into real file- and project-based workflows.
  • ChatGPT 101: Introduction to ChatGPT for Small Businesses — Free; beginner; suitable for small-business use and role-based recommendations.
  • Resource Guides for Small Business Videos — Free; beginner-plus; rich in practical prompt examples.
